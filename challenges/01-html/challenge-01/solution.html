The Evolution of Modern Web Standards

Published on September 23, 2026 by Jane Doe

In the early days of the web, developers used tables for layout. Later, developers transitioned to div-based layouts. Today, semantic HTML allows browsers, search engines, and screen readers to clearly understand the intent and meaning of every block of content.

Semantic tags improve accessibility by allowing screen readers to jump between landmarks with single keystrokes.
